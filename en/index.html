Educational tools

ORBIT↗

A satellite mission studio: configure an original spacecraft, explore a circular orbit, reveal its construction, and compare locally saved studies.

PRODUCT DESIGN TOOLS

RESON↗

An original loudspeaker design studio. Configure materials and proportions, explore the construction in 3D, and compare named alternatives saved in your browser.

INTERACTIVE PRODUCT STUDIES

LUMEN↗

An original optical lens study with a moving iris, an opening case, nine-group scroll-driven separation and a complete return to the assembled form.

REVIEW SYSTEMS

GuideCheck↗

Compare guide revisions and keep human review evidence attached to the exact steps it belongs to.

FILE INTEGRITY

ArchiveGuard↗

Compare JPEG capture dates with Google Photos sidecars, resolve exceptions, and export verified new copies.

Navigation and accessibility

ITER↗

An educational navigator for a fictitious building. Passage constraints, a 3D model, an SVG plan and written steps describe the same route.

Local applications

AYQYN↗

A Windows local proctoring prototype: participant consent, camera preparation, on-device observations and a separate human review of events.

Musical instruments

SIDEREAL↗

A musical observatory in the browser. A star’s position sets its timing and pitch; an orbital cursor turns the drawing into a four-bar composition.
