## Material and type observations

- Petals are broad, concave and softly irregular, with thin curled edges, fine directional surface detail and blush-pink tips. The bases are warm ivory; the shadows carry a restrained pale green tint. They are neither rigid wedges nor metallic shells.
- The center is small and organic. It should not become a heavy plinth, mechanical hub or a high-cost decoration that dominates the actual navigation.
- The ground is pale warm neutral. Broad soft contact shadows locate the flower; no strong glow, hard outline or glossy black contrast is visible.
- The headline is a large italic serif, centered above the flower. The signature and petal names are restrained, spaced uppercase serif text; language controls and small warm-pink underlines are quiet. Typography should remain semantic HTML with complete Cyrillic/Kazakh coverage.
- These are observed relationships, not sampled hexadecimal color values or a claim of a newly approved exact palette. Final material/light choices must be judged on the actual generated geometry in the site.
- The illustrated furniture, mannequin and boards are storyboard concept art. They must not replace real PERCH, SELVEDGE and FORME screenshots or evidence.

## Initial scaffold gaps (historical)

The state/history/reader/localization foundation is usable, but its wireframe outlines, five framed labels and generic side preview do not yet meet this composition. Wireframe imagery is explicitly temporary. Its performance cannot stand in for generated-model performance.

The final scene needs the single approved Tripo master petal, then local Blender work for five variations and continuous soft bloom. Check its thickness, concavity, base, normals and edge at 0%, 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% bloom. Preserve the source output, editable Blender file, optimized GLBs and generation receipt. Model acceptance must happen inside this scene, with actual project surfaces and camera framing.

Mobile needs a separately composed active petal, readable labels and ordinary touch scrolling. Reducing the desktop composition proportionally is insufficient. The semantic index remains available throughout asset loading, failure and reduced motion.
